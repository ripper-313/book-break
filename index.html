Get started with Book Break

Keep it simple

Your book, your pace

No assigned chapters and no catching up. Bring a novel, comic, or anything else you're into.

A quiet half hour

We start with 30 minutes of reading. Talking afterward is optional, so you can just hang out and read.

Something new to read

Ask what someone else brought or share a quick reccomendation. Doesn't have to be a whole book review.

Start with a reading hour

The idea is to set aside one hour for reading instead of scrolling. Read quietly for the first half, then stick around to talk about your book or hear what other people picked.

What to bring

  • A book, comic, or e-reader
  • A drink, if you feel like it
  • An extra book to swap (optional)

A couple more ideas

Bring a book to swap

Bring a book you're done with and let someone else try it. You don't have to take one.

Read the bring-along list

Dont know what to read?

Try a short story or ask someone what they read recently. Pretty much any genre is fine.
